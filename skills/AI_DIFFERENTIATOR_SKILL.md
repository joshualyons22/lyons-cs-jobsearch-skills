## AUDIENCE-SPECIFIC FRAMING

### Non-Technical Hiring Manager (VP of CS, HR, Director of Revenue)

Lead with outcomes and time savings. They care about efficiency, customer experience, and organizational impact — not the technical architecture.

**Key messages:**
- Lead with: "I wasn't trying to be a developer. I was trying to spend less time on logistics and more time on customers."
- Focus on: specific time savings (X minutes per meeting, X hours per month), customer experience improvements, peer adoption
- Land on: "Most CSMs talk about using AI. I've shipped tools that my peers use every day."

**What to avoid:**
- Technical terminology
- Details about the build process or toolchain that don't add meaning
- Anything that sounds like you were doing the engineering team's job

---

### Technical Hiring Manager or AI-Curious CS Leader

Lead with the builder mindset and the iteration process.

**Key messages:**
- "[Your honest framing of your technical depth — e.g., 'I don't have a formal development background — I learned through curiosity and iteration.']"
- Mention your toolchain: [List the actual tools you used — e.g., Claude, Cursor, Zapier, specific MCP servers]
- Land on: "Iteration is as important in AI as it is in any business process. The tools I built later were significantly better than what I built first."

**What to avoid:**
- Overstating technical depth — be honest that you're a practitioner, not a developer
- Underselling the impact — organizational adoption is genuinely impressive regardless of technical sophistication

---

### The Skeptic

Don't lead with defense. Lead with curiosity about their skepticism.

**Approach:**
1. Seek to understand: "What's driving that reaction for you?"
2. Validate if appropriate: "That's a fair concern — I had similar reservations before I started building."
3. Bridge: "What I found is that the value wasn't immediately obvious until I saw what it did to my actual workflow."
4. Once they're engaged: share specific outcomes

**Key reframe:**
The skeptic usually objects to AI hype, not AI utility. Agree with them on the hype, then show them the utility.
